MEASUREMENT NOTES

The definitions behind the numbers

Churn probability
Estimated likelihood of no completed purchase in the next 90 days for an eligible customer.
Revenue exposure
Prior 180-day revenue ÷ 2 × churn probability. A probability-weighted historical revenue proxy, not a forecast of causal loss.
Risk bands
High and Medium thresholds are chosen using validation data.
Sales forecast
Six monthly periods ahead, selected with rolling-origin evaluation. Intervals are approximate and based on historical forecast errors.
No future information
Customer features use purchases strictly before each snapshot. Outcome labels use the subsequent fully observed 90-day window.
From signals to decisions
Recommended actions use explicit behavioral rules. Test interventions with a holdout group and track incremental margin, opt-outs, and repeat purchases.
